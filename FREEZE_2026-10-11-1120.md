## What changed
A CPR trigger that lands on the latest COMPLETED 5-minute candle now fires at the
entry instant (the next candle's open, +5m) instead of waiting a further 5
minutes for that candle to complete. The Telegram alert time now equals the entry
time shown in the message (published "Signal HH:MM").

- `app/live/engine.py`
  - `_cpr_book_signal`: when the trigger is on the latest completed candle, the
    entry candle is the one forming at `completed+5m`; its OPEN is used as the
    entry (was: `entry_at_next_open` -> WAIT for 5 more minutes).
  - `_default_candles`: includes the candle that opens at `completed+5m` so the
    forming-entry candle is available.
  - `evaluate`: completed-candle gates (session, integrity, market_state) still
    see completed candles only; the forming candle is used solely as the entry
    OPEN; any candle beyond `completed+5m` is still rejected as `FUTURE_CANDLE`.
- `tests/test_phase9.py`, `tests/test_phase11.py`: the injected future candle
  moved from `completed+5m` to `completed+10m` (completed+5m is now the
  legitimate forming entry). No trade-value assertions were changed.
- `frontend/signal-flow.html`: timing note and worked example updated to
  alert == entry.
- crontab: `tg_alerts.py --watch` cadence `*/5 9-15` -> `* 9-15` (Mon-Fri) so the
  alert fires on the earliest minute at/after the entry instant.

## Explicitly UNCHANGED (trade logic)
- Trigger rules: bear-first, R2/R1/PDH/BELOW_CPR, S2/S1/PDL/ABOVE_CPR, weekly
  gate (aligned), first-trigger-of-day.
- Entry/stop/target math: 0.5% stop / 2% target, BEAR/BULL branch.
- Entry window: [09:20, 15:20).
- `signal_time` = trigger candle, `entry_time` = next candle open (+5m).
- No lookahead: only the forming candle's OPEN is read (never H/L/C); the trigger
  scan still runs over completed candles only.
