## What changed
Follow-up to FREEZE_2026-10-11-1150 (intrabar entry + frozen trade). The live
engine already exposes the frozen entry instant as `entry_time` on the trade
dict returned by `/api/decision`; several surfaces still read the paper trigger's
`signal_time` (next-open). Aligned them to prefer the live/frozen trade.

- `scripts/tg_alerts.py` (`:356`): Telegram "Signal HH:MM" now prefers live
  `t.entry_time` -> `t.signal_time` -> paper `_tr.entry_time` -> `_tr.signal_time`.
- `frontend/index.html` (homepage live-alert card `alertCard`): now fetches
  `/api/decision/<slug>` alongside `/api/paper/spread/today`; when
  `decision === 'TRADE'` it uses the live/frozen trade's `direction`, `entry`,
  `stop` and `entry_time`, else falls back to the paper trigger. The
  "Signal HH:MM" label now shows the entry instant.
- `frontend/indices/nifty.html`, `frontend/indices/banknifty.html`
  ("Today's model record" table): same change — fetch `/api/decision/<slug>`
  too; Direction / Entry (ref) / SL / Signal time prefer the live/frozen trade
  values, else the paper trigger.
- `app/decision/view.py` (`:313,315`): narrative reworded to intrabar —
  trigger "CPR level touch fires the <strategy> entry at <entry> (intrabar)";
  entry_zone "Entered at the firing instant near <entry>". (Dropped the now
  inaccurate "5-minute candle closes toward ...".)
