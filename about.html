About TradingAI

Free, independent market research for the Indian stock, index and options markets.

What TradingAI does

TradingAI is an automated market-intelligence platform for Indian traders and long-term investors. It analyses NSE index and stock data throughout the trading day and presents that reading in plain language: is the market trending or range-bound, where are the key support and resistance levels, what is volatility doing, and which option strikes carry the heaviest open interest. The site organises its output around real, observable inputs — price, volume, breadth, VWAP, RSI, ADX, VIX, put-call ratios, and max-pain — so that every outlook or strategy shown can be traced back to the numbers that produced it.

Content areas include: intraday index outlook (NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, FINNIFTY, SENSEX), the broad-market dashboard, an equity scanner, options strategies with a payoff calculator, PCR and max-pain analysis, mutual fund snapshots, and a free education library.

How the analysis is produced

Everything on the site is generated by software, not by individual editorial opinion. A pipeline fetches intraday prices and EOD bhavcopy files from public Indian market data sources (chiefly the National Stock Exchange of India, with alternative feeds as backups), stores them, computes technical indicators, classifies the prevailing market regime, evaluates standard options strategies, and publishes the result through the open APIs that power these pages. Where an AI model is involved, it is used to summarise the indicator output into readable paragraphs — the underlying numbers always come from the market data itself.

This automated, rule-based approach is what makes TradingAI free and consistent: every visitor looks at the same transparent, reproducible analysis rather than paid recommendations.

What TradingAI is not

This is not a brokerage, a portfolio manager, a paid subscription service, or a trading signal service. TradingAI does not hold, trade, or custody any client funds, and it never asks for your broker credentials or bank details. The content is decision-support and education, not investment advice, and no page on the site guarantees results. Final responsibility for every trade — entry, sizing, stop-loss and exit — rests with you. Please read the disclaimer and terms of service.

Data sources and independence

TradingAI is an independent research project. Index levels, option open interest, corporate actions and fund NAVs are drawn from publicly available sources such as NSE India and AMFI. TradingAI is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or compensated by the National Stock Exchange, AMFI, or any brokerage. Market data may be delayed or inaccurate; always verify with your broker before acting.
