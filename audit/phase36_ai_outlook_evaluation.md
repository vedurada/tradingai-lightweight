These market outlooks are generated by **rule-based replay** (ai_source: RULE_REPLAY),
NOT by the Groq LLM. They represent deterministic rule-based outlooks, not AI predictions.

Therefore, accuracy metrics from this evaluation reflect rule-based outlook quality,
NOT AI prediction accuracy.

## Sample Size Warning
27 outlooks over 27 trading days is a small sample. No statistical significance
can be claimed. This evaluation is descriptive, not inferential.

## Limitations
