Contact

We read every message — questions, corrections, feature ideas, and partnerships.

Email

The fastest way to reach TradingAI is by email: info@tradingai.in. We typically respond within a few business days.

For quick feedback on a specific page, mention the page URL and the data you believe is incorrect, and we will investigate the source feed.

What to include

To help us help you:

  • Questions about analysis: include the page or symbol you are looking at (for example "NIFTY outlook on indices/nifty.html").
  • Corrections: state what you expected, what you saw, and the approximate time — we audit our data pipeline regularly.
  • Feature requests: a one-line idea is fine; be as specific as you like.
  • Partnerships / advertising: please use the subject "Advertising" or "Partnership".

Response times

Email is monitored on working days (Indian Standard Time). Because TradingAI is an independent site, response times can vary — if your message is urgent, please allow up to 5 business days before following up.
