How to read this page

The Holdings Explorer drills into what an index ETF actually owns. Knowing the constituent weights matters because an ETF is only as diversified as its biggest names: a handful of stocks often represent a third or more of a broad index fund's value.

The table lists each holding's symbol, name and percentage of the fund, ordered by weight. The bigger the top name, the more the ETF's price tracks that single stock — a fact that matters when you are using the ETF as a "low-risk core" position. GOLDBEES is different: its holdings are bullion-backed and its price is driven by the international gold rate, not by equities, so compare it against gold charts rather than the NIFTY.

Holdings are refreshed weekly from the fund's public disclosures, so the breakdown is a recent snapshot rather than a real-time view. Fund managers rebalance, and exact percentages shift between disclosure dates.

This information is for research. It is not a recommendation to buy or sell any ETF, and past weightings do not predict future performance.
