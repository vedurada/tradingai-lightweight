How to read this page

This page ranks the ETFs TradingAI tracks by live performance. ETFs open a cheap, liquid way to participate in a market theme — index, sector, gold or international equity — and this listing shows their movement at a glance so you can compare assets with very different return profiles side by side.

A useful habit is to compare an index ETF's move with the index page for the same benchmark. If the ETF is drifting away from its NAV or its underlying, liquidity and tracking error deserve a look. The same applies to gold or international ETFs, where currency and global hours add another layer.

Every row links to the symbol's research page so you can read the technical context behind the price, and the Learn hub explains how ETFs, premium/discount and tracking error work for investors new to these products.

ETF prices here come from public feeds and can be delayed or approximate. Nothing on this page is a recommendation to buy or sell a fund. Compare NAV, expense ratio, and liquidity on the AMC's own site before investing — educational context only.
