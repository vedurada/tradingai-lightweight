How to read this archive

A reference trigger is the level the model classified a record at; the invalidation and target are the rule-defined levels that followed it. A favourable reference outcome means the session reached the target reference before the session closed; adverse means it reached the invalidation first; session exits mean neither reference level was reached and the record was closed at the session boundary. All figures are model references, not fills, and include no costs. Rules and assumptions are documented in the model methodology; the longer observation ledger is on the observations journal.
