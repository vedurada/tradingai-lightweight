Trade Definition

A qualified trade requires: CONFIRMED scenario + valid strategy + risk validation (1% max risk, 1.5x min R:R) + daily limit (1/instrument/day) + options data available. Entry = price * 0.995, Stop = entry * 0.99, Target = entry * 1.02.

Daily Trade Limit

MAX_QUALIFIED_TRADES_PER_DAY = 1 per instrument per trading date. Maximum possible trades = number of trading sessions.
