Base trigger strategy (Plain variant): bull on S2/S1/PDL touch or close above TC; bear on R2/R1/PDH touch or close below BC. Aligned adds the weekly-CPR gate and is the default view. First trigger per session, entry at next open in [09:20,15:10), 0.5% stop / 2% target evaluated at subsequent opens (stop first), flat 15:10 open. Sub-30pt (NIFTY) / sub-60pt (BANKNIFTY) adverse drifts count as decay wins. Read-only over stored candles; paper days append below as they accumulate.
