What does this mean?

The Central Pivot Range (CPR) is the band between the previous session’s central pivot support and resistance, with the pivot in the middle. BANKNIFTY intraday traders watch whether price is above, inside or below this range. Today’s CPR values and classification appear here when market data is available.

New to BANKNIFTY options?

A learning sequence using the explainers already on this site.

  1. Understand CPR — the day’s central pivot range.
  2. Understand support & resistance — where price tends to react.
  3. Learn the trading session and when BANKNIFTY actually trades.
  4. Understand the option chain & open interest.
  5. Learn PCR — put/call ratio as a positioning measure.
  6. Understand Max Pain.
  7. Understand expected move and India VIX.
  8. Learn defined-risk strategies — bull put spread and bear call spread — so risk is capped in advance.
  9. Practise on historical data before risking anything.
