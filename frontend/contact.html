About TradingAI.in

TradingAI.in is an independent, rules-based market-structure dashboard for Indian indices (NIFTY, BANKNIFTY) and volatility (INDIA VIX). It classifies intraday structure — CPR levels, triggers, paper-tracked outcomes — and publishes both its signals and its full track record. It is a research and educational tool: it offers no brokerage, manages no money, and gives no personalised advice.
