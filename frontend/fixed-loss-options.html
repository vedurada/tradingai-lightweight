Strategy templates

How to read the payoff chart

The chart plots profit or loss at expiry against the underlying's closing price. Points above zero are gains, below zero are losses; where the line crosses zero are your breakevens. Because every template here combines bought and sold legs, the line always flattens outside the wings — that flattening is the fixed, predefined risk you are studying. Toggle the expiry and volatility inputs to see how time and IV reshape the curve before expiry: longer expiries and higher IV inflate both option values, which helps debit structures and hurts credit structures, all else equal.

What each template is for

  • Bull Call Spread (debit): buy ATM call, sell higher call. Capped gain if the index closes above the short strike; max loss is the debit paid. Studied for measured upside participation with a hard floor on loss.
  • Bear Put Spread (debit): the mirror — buy ATM put, sell lower put. Capped gain below the short strike; max loss the debit.
  • Bull Put Spread (credit): sell OTM put, buy a lower put as protection. Keeps the credit if price stays above the short strike; max loss is the wing width minus credit. The structure behind this site's BULL observations (bull put spread guide).
  • Bear Call Spread (credit): sell OTM call, buy a higher call. Keeps the credit below the short strike; max loss wing width minus credit. The structure behind BEAR observations (bear call spread guide).
  • Iron Condor (credit): a bear call spread plus a bull put spread — profits when the index pins inside both short strikes, defined loss outside either wing. A range-study structure, fragile in trends.
  • Butterfly (debit): two wings bought around two sold ATM options — peak gain only near the middle strike, small defined loss elsewhere. A pinning-study structure.
  • Straddle (debit): ATM call plus ATM put — profits only on a large move either way beyond the combined premium. The physical form of the expected move.

Assumptions and limits of this sketch

  • Payoffs are expiry payoffs from intrinsic value; live P&L before expiry differs with time decay, IV shifts and bid-ask spreads.
  • Premiums here are modelled, not market quotes — use this builder to learn shapes, and your broker's chain for real prices.
  • Brokerage, STT, stamp duty, GST and slippage are excluded; on narrow spreads these frictions decide whether a theoretical edge survives.
  • Lot sizes, expiries and margin rules follow the exchange's current circulars — verify before studying any structure against live data.
