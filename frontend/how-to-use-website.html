The narrative — Ravi's six stops (words to match the motion)

1 · Outlook. Home shows three cards — NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, India VIX — each with a timestamp. Outside 09:15–15:30 IST they show end-of-day context. Start here: home.
2 · Inputs. Each card lists observed inputs — vs VWAP, CPR position, gap — and what would change it. Full definitions: methodology.
3 · Levels. Index pages show reference trigger, invalidation and target — the same numbers behind every alert. Study, don't chase: NIFTY, BANKNIFTY.
4 · Learn. Spread guides explain payoff, breakeven and capped loss with assumed premiums: Bull Put, Bear Call, compared.
5 · Records. Every alert is kept win or lose: alerts archive and observations journal. Check adverse days first.
6 · Decide. Size with risk calculator, log five lines in journal. Your call — always. Education, not advice.

Five-line journal (copy daily)

  1. Date + CPR width (narrow / normal / wide).
  2. Morning map: TC / Pivot / BC / S1 / R1 / high / low.
  3. Gap in one sentence.
  4. First qualifying close — or "no classified session".
  5. Outcome vs the predefined line + one lesson.
