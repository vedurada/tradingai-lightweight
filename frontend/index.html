Market outlook

What this outlook covers

TradingAI.in publishes a daily educational market outlook for three instruments — NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, and India VIX — built from transparent, rule-based inputs: price position vs VWAP, Central Pivot Range (CPR) levels from the previous session, opening gap direction, and the prevailing market regime. Each card shows its data timestamp in IST and whether the feed is live, delayed, or closed for the day.

Classification runs during NSE market hours (09:15–15:30 IST, Monday–Friday): completed 5-minute candles are evaluated against the published signal flowchart, and one hypothetical observation per instrument per day is recorded for study in the observations journal and alerts archive. All rules, inputs, and limitations are documented in the methodology, and key options concepts are explained in Learn. Content is general education for studying market context — never investment advice, and it never considers your financial circumstances.

How to read this replay

Each replay compresses one classified session into six steps. Press play on a card, or jump with the numbered dots.

  • Yesterday settles — the blue band is the Central Pivot Range (top/bottom central) computed from the previous session only.
  • Morning gap — where the index opened versus the previous close, and which way the gap leaned.
  • Five-minute candles watch — the dark line is the session price path, sampled from recorded 5-minute closes starting 09:15.
  • Trigger — the candle that accepted beyond a CPR boundary and classified the session (BEAR below support, BULL above resistance).
  • Reference levels — the dashed lines the rules attached to the record: trigger, invalidation, reference target, and the spread studied.
  • Outcome — how the record closed at the session boundary, kept in the alerts archive win or lose.

Levels are rounded for display and the path is simplified; exact figures live on the record cards in the archive. Replays refresh every trading evening. Educational sketches only — never investment advice.
