India VIX Explained for Index-Options Traders

India VIX measures the market's expectation of near-term NIFTY volatility implied by option prices. TradingAI tracks it as a live instrument and shows a volatility-regime context on the homepage — high VIX means options are pricing bigger swings, not predicting direction. A rising VIX does not always mean NIFTY will fall.

Educational guide only. Metrics are analytical references, not personalised investment advice. Options involve substantial risk; see the Disclaimer.

1. What India VIX is

  • Computed from NIFTY option premiums: the more traders pay for options, the higher the VIX.
  • Quoted in annualised percentage terms; roughly, VIX ÷ √252 approximates a daily one-sigma move in percent.

2. What volatility represents

  • Volatility is the size of expected moves, with no sign attached — high volatility means bigger up and down days.
  • Realised volatility (what happened) vs implied volatility (what options price) — persistent gaps between them are what premium sellers and buyers live on.

3. High vs low volatility context

  • Low VIX: cheap options, compressed ranges; breakouts from low-volatility regimes are often violent when they come.
  • High VIX: expensive options, wide intraday swings; premium sellers demand more edge, buyers should expect whipsaws.
  • Context is relative: compare VIX to its own recent range, not to an absolute number.

4. VIX, options and Expected Move

  • VIX is the headline number behind the Expected Move: higher VIX mechanically widens it.
  • VIX spikes often coincide with PCR extremes driven by hedging demand — read them together (PCR guide).

5. How TradingAI uses VIX

  • INDIA_VIX 5-minute candles feed the homepage volatility card and regime context next to NIFTY/BANKNIFTY model output.
  • VIX informs the environment (position sizing caution, spread-width context), never the outlook direction — outlook direction comes from CPR classifications.

6. Limitations

  • VIX can stay low while single-session shocks still occur, and stay high during grinding non-trends that defeat both buyers and sellers.
  • Never use VIX as a directional timing signal on its own.
