Methodology: Data, Rules and Limitations

TradingAI.in is an educational Indian market analytics platform. All outputs are deterministic evaluations of the published rules below — no language model generates trading decisions or personalised advice. TradingAI.in does not provide investment advice. Nothing here considers your financial circumstances.

1. Data sources

  • Index and volatility prices and 5-minute candles: public market feeds (including Yahoo Finance series for NIFTY, BANKNIFTY and India VIX).
  • Options reference data: exchange option-chain snapshots when reachable; the interface states availability explicitly.
  • All timestamps are Indian Standard Time (IST, Asia/Kolkata).

2. Data refresh frequency

  • Live context refreshes on short polling intervals during market hours (09:15–15:30 IST, Monday–Friday).
  • Outside market hours, cards show end-of-day or historical context with the data timestamp.
  • Historical ledgers refresh once per session after close.

4. Inputs used by the rule-based classification

  • Price versus VWAP (5-minute cumulative typical-price reference).
  • Opening range position and gap type versus previous close.
  • CPR relationship: price above TC / inside CPR / below BC, and CPR width (narrow / normal / wide).
  • Market breadth and volume context where available.
  • India VIX condition and trend/range condition.

5. Missing-data treatment

  • Missing inputs are omitted from the observed-inputs list, never defaulted.
  • If classification is impossible, the card shows No Clear Outlook or Insufficient Data.
  • Unavailable values display as “—”, “unavailable”, or “Loading market context…”, never as zero or a neutral reading.

6. How classifications are computed (observational)

  • Previous-session high/low/close produce the CPR framework (Pivot, TC, BC, S1/S2, R1/R2) plus previous day high/low.
  • Completed 5-minute candles are compared against the framework; the first classified observation of the day anchors the session context.
  • Confidence (Low / Moderate / High) reflects agreement among available inputs, not a probability of any market move.

7. Options learning content

  • Strategy pages are educational illustrations: structure, theoretical payoff shape, break-even concept, maximum theoretical loss, and risk factors.
  • Illustrative premiums are assumed for teaching and are not live prices; nothing suggests using any structure on any day.
  • Background: defined-risk option structures, Bull Put Spread, Bear Call Spread.

8. Historical-analysis assumptions

  • Backtests run on stored 5-minute candles with no-lookahead evaluation.
  • Cost treatment varies by view; treat results as gross of your actual brokerage, taxes and slippage unless a view states otherwise.
  • Historical and hypothetical outcomes do not predict future results. See historical scenario analysis.

9. Model limitations

  • Rules simplify markets; gaps, news shocks and illiquidity regularly produce behaviour outside any classification.
  • SENSEX coverage is planned; until then its card shows Insufficient Data — coverage planned.
  • Options reference fields appear only when the exchange feed is reachable.

10. No-future-prediction statement

TradingAI.in does not predict future prices, does not recommend transactions, and does not consider any user's circumstances. All labels, levels and historical views are for study.

11. Model alerts and what they are

TradingAI.in publishes a fixed, rule-based model output to a public messaging channel (morning reference levels, classified model records, invalidation and end-of-day exit alerts, and an end-of-day recap). Subscription is offered on the home, NIFTY and BANKNIFTY outlook pages; every alert is also kept permanently in the alert archive on this site. These are hypothetical, automated, deterministic evaluations of published rules on public data, published win or lose. They are educational observations for study, not personalised advice, and the site sends no SMS, email or browser notification.
