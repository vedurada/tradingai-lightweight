Methodology

How TradingAI analyzes markets, identifies scenarios, and qualifies trades.

Pipeline

1. Historical Context

Previous completed sessions are analyzed for price-action patterns, trends, volatility regimes, and structural characteristics.

2. Scenario Generation

Candidate scenarios (bullish continuation, range, breakout, etc.) are generated from historical features.

3. Current Confirmation

Today market data is matched against scenarios. A scenario confirms only after current conditions validate it.

4. Options and Risk

Valid options data is required. Risk engine evaluates entry, stop, target, and reward/risk ratio.

5. Deterministic Qualification

The deterministic engine decides QUALIFIED TRADE or NO TRADE. AI never overrides this decision.

6. One Trade Per Day

Server-side lock enforces maximum one qualified trade per day. After qualification, the lock is consumed.

7. Paper Trading

Every qualified trade becomes a paper trade. Monitoring, exit, and outcome tracking follow.

8. AI Explanation

AI explains why a scenario matched, why a trade qualified, and what risks exist. AI never changes the decision.

No-Look-Ahead

For every decision at timestamp T, only data available at or before T is used. Future candles, outcomes, and information are never used in any decision or backtest.

Limitations

Historical scenarios are observations, not predictions. Backtest results are research data, not guarantees. Paper trades are simulations. AI availability may vary.
