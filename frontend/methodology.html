Methodology

TradingAI is a deterministic market-intelligence system. It does not predict markets or force trades. It analyzes historical and current data to identify scenarios and evaluate whether conditions support a trade.

Market Data

Market data is sourced from yfinance for NIFTY 50, BANKNIFTY 50, and India VIX. All timestamps use Asia/Kolkata timezone. Data freshness is tracked and stale data is flagged clearly. Unavailable data is never replaced with defaults.

Historical Scenario Analysis

Previous completed sessions are analyzed for price-action patterns. Candidate scenarios are generated based on defined rules:

Study the Past→Identify patterns
Generate candidates→BULLISH_CONTINUATION, BEARISH_CONTINUATION, RANGE_PREMIUM_DECAY, BREAKOUT, BREAKOUT_FAILURE_REVERSAL

Current-Day Confirmation

Historical similarity alone never creates a trade. Each scenario must be confirmed by current market data:

Current 5m data→Market state assessment
Scenario matching→WATCH → PARTIALLY_MATCHED → CONFIRMED / INVALIDATED
Confirmation→Only CONFIRMED scenarios proceed

Options & Strategy Selection

Once a scenario is confirmed, the strategy engine selects a strategy based on direction and volatility:

BULLISH + HIGH vol→Bull Call Spread
BULLISH + LOW vol→Bull Put Spread
BEARISH + HIGH vol→Bear Put Spread
RANGE→Iron Condor

Options data must be validated before any trade. If unavailable: NO TRADE.

Risk Qualification

Every candidate must pass risk checks: entry/stop/target validity, minimum reward:risk ratio (1.5:1), maximum risk per trade (2%). All rejection reasons are stored and displayed.

One-Trade-Per-Day Lock

The database enforces a hard limit of one qualified trade per instrument per day. This is server-side only — frontend cannot bypass it.

Paper Trading

Qualified trades become paper trades monitored in real-time. Exit reasons include: TARGET, STOP, SCENARIO_INVALIDATED, TIME_EXIT, END_OF_DAY, RISK_EXIT, DATA_INVALID.

Backtesting

Historical replay runs in complete isolation from live data. Every decision enforces the no-look-ahead rule: actual_latest_data ≤ decision_timestamp. Results include full trade journal, outcome metrics, and validation status.

AI Explanation Layer

AI explains decisions but never makes them. Running with AI enabled vs disabled produces identical trade decisions. If AI is unavailable, the deterministic engine continues unchanged.

Limitations

  • Options data currently unavailable — options-dependent trades return NO TRADE
  • yfinance data may have delays for Indian indices
  • Backtest outcomes are simulated from candle data
  • No live options chain data is available yet
