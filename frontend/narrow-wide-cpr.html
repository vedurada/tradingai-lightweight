Narrow vs Wide CPR Strategy for Intraday Trading

CPR width — the distance between the Top Central Pivot (TC) and Bottom Central Pivot (BC) relative to price — describes how undecided the previous NIFTY or BANKNIFTY session was. Narrow and wide CPR days tend to produce different intraday behaviour: breakout expansion versus rotation inside the range. This guide explains both and how the TradingAI model treats each day.

1. How CPR width is classified

Width (TC−BC)/Pivot: narrow below 0.15%, normal between 0.15% and 0.30%, wide above 0.30%. TradingAI computes this every morning from the previous session and labels the day on the NIFTY and BANKNIFTY model pages. The full calculation is in the CPR guide.

2. Narrow CPR days: breakout behaviour

  • A tight TC/BC zone means yesterday balanced on a narrow range — stored energy that often releases as a directional move once price closes outside the zone.
  • Commonly watched sequence: price pushes above TC or below BC, then a completed 5-minute candle closes outside the zone (confirmation), which is the region the classification rules evaluate most closely.
  • Risk note: narrow days also produce false starts — price pokes out and falls back inside. That is why the model requires a finished-candle close, not a wick touch, for TC/BC breaks.

3. Wide CPR days: rotation behaviour

  • A broad undecided zone often acts as a magnet: price rotates between its edges, reacting at TC from below and BC from above.
  • S1/S2 and R1/R2 outside the zone become the extension references if rotation fails and range breaks.
  • On wide days the model frequently outputs WAIT or NO TRADE while price stays inside — that restraint is part of the design, visible across the historical model observations.

5. Illustrative example

Illustrative example: NIFTY prints a narrow CPR (TC/BC 0.08% apart). Price opens flat, pushes above TC at 10:00 and a 5-minute candle closes above it — an upward-leaning observation of the type the classification rules evaluate. Contrast a wide day (0.45% apart) where price oscillates between TC and BC until noon with no classified observation — a day typically recorded as having no classified session.
