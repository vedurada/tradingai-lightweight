NIFTY & BANKNIFTY Support & Resistance via CPR

Each trading day, the previous NIFTY and BANKNIFTY session converts into seven reference levels: the CPR trio (BC, Pivot, TC), two supports (S1, S2), two resistances (R1, R2), plus the previous day high and low. This page explains how those daily trading levels are built and how reactions at each are commonly read intraday.

Educational references only — levels describe past-derived structure, not personalised investment advice. For live values see the NIFTY and BANKNIFTY model pages. Options involve substantial risk; see the Disclaimer.

2. Reading reactions: touch vs acceptance

  • A touch (wick reaching a level) shows the level is recognised; a close beyond on a completed 5-minute candle shows acceptance — the distinction the TradingAI classification rules are built on.
  • First tests of virgin levels carry the most information; retests later in the day carry less.
  • Confluence strengthens a reference: e.g. S1 coinciding with the previous day low, or R1 with the weekly CPR boundary.

3. Illustrative example

Illustrative example: NIFTY's map shows S1 near 24,100. Price dips to S1 mid-morning and a 5-minute candle closes back above it — a hold of the type upward-leaning classification logic looks for. Had the candle instead closed below BC with follow-through, that would be weakness of the type bearish logic evaluates. Neither event alone is a recommendation; both are evidence inputs.
