Option Chain & Open Interest Guide for NIFTY & BANKNIFTY

An option chain lists every strike's calls and puts with their open interest (OI), volume and premiums. For NIFTY and BANKNIFTY traders it is the positioning map: where the market has placed its biggest bets. TradingAI reads chain contracts through a validated data layer whenever the exchange feed is reachable.

Educational guide only. Metrics are analytical references, not personalised investment advice. Options involve substantial risk; see the Disclaimer.

1. Call OI, Put OI and change in OI

  • Open Interest = outstanding contracts not yet closed — a stock of positioning. Volume = contracts traded today — a flow.
  • Change in OI shows where positions were added today: rising OI with rising premium suggests fresh writing/buying conviction; rising OI with falling premium often means writers pressing.
  • Always read OI together with price: OI without price action is just inventory.

2. OI concentration

  • Concentration = a few strikes holding most of the chain's OI. Concentrated strikes act as gravitational zones into expiry.
  • Concentration that shifts intraday (writers defending a new strike) matters more than static totals.

3. Call concentration vs put concentration

  • Heavy call OI above spot often marks a supply zone (resistance); heavy put OI below spot often marks a demand zone (support).
  • These are positioning references for support & resistance, complementing CPR levels — not guarantees that levels will hold.

4. Support/resistance interpretation

  • Confluence counts: a CPR level (CPR guide) sitting on top of heavy OI concentration is a stronger reference than either alone.
  • Watch for OI unwinding (falling OI into a level break) — it often confirms the break better than the break itself.

5. Limitations

  • OI does not reveal direction of the holder (long vs short) or motive (hedge vs speculation).
  • Weekly-expiry rolls, event hedging and intraday squaring can redraw the map within hours.
  • Stale chains mislead: TradingAI validates contracts and marks freshness; unavailable data is shown as unavailable, never zero-filled.
