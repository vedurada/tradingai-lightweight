PCR Explained for NIFTY & BANKNIFTY Options

The Put-Call Ratio (PCR) compares open interest in puts against open interest in calls for an index option chain. On TradingAI.in it is one of the positioning references recorded in NIFTY and BANKNIFTY options snapshots whenever the exchange feed is reachable.

Educational guide only. Metrics are analytical references, not personalised investment advice. Options involve substantial risk; see the Disclaimer.

1. What PCR is

  • Conceptually PCR = total Put OI ÷ total Call OI across the chain (or a chosen expiry).
  • PCR above 1 means more put open interest than call open interest; below 1 means the reverse.
  • It is a positioning snapshot, not a price forecast — it describes where traders have placed bets, not where the index must go.

2. How traders interpret PCR

  • Very high PCR is often read as heavy put writing/hedging (sometimes contrarian-bullish); very low PCR as heavy call exposure.
  • The informative movement is usually the change in PCR through the session, read together with price action — rising PCR while price holds support differs from rising PCR while price breaks down.
  • There is no universal threshold: a PCR above any fixed number is not automatically bullish, and no level guarantees direction.

3. Why PCR must not be used alone

  • PCR says nothing about who holds the positions (writers vs buyers) or why (hedge vs speculation).
  • Expiry-day unwinds, rollovers and event hedging can distort PCR without any directional meaning.
  • Always combine PCR with price location (CPR levels), OI concentration by strike, VIX context and the market regime.

4. PCR with price action, OI and VIX

  • PCR + price: PCR extreme at a CPR support/resistance level is more interesting than the number alone — see the support & resistance guide.
  • PCR + OI concentration: check which strikes carry the OI in the option chain guide; a lopsided PCR driven by one far strike means little.
  • PCR + VIX: PCR extremes during volatility expansions often reflect hedging demand rather than conviction — see the India VIX guide.

5. PCR on TradingAI.in

  • TradingAI records PCR in its options snapshots when the exchange feed is reachable. When it is not, the interface shows an unavailable state instead of a number — a missing PCR is never replaced with 0 or a neutral reading.
