Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026. TradingAI.in is an educational market analytics platform. This policy explains what information is collected when you use it.

Information we collect

  • No accounts, no tracking profiles. The site offers no login and sets no advertising or analytics cookies of its own (third-party analytics/advertising cookies are described below).
  • Server logs. Like nearly every website, the server records standard technical entries (requested page, time, approximate network origin) for security and reliability. Logs are rotated and not sold or shared.
  • Market data. Price displays come from public market-data providers; requesting a page causes the server to fetch public quotes on your behalf. No personal data is sent to those providers.

Cookies and analytics

  • The site uses Google Analytics (GA4) to understand aggregate usage (pages viewed, approximate region, device type). Google sets cookies such as _ga and _ga_* for this purpose; data is processed under Google's privacy policy and retained per the property's settings.
  • You can opt out with Google's analytics opt-out add-on or by blocking third-party cookies in your browser.

Advertising

  • This site may display ads served by Google AdSense and its partners. Google uses cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other sites, which may include personalized advertising.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ad Settings or aboutads.info. See also how Google uses data from partner sites.

How information is used

  • Operating, securing and improving the dashboard; diagnosing errors; complying with legal obligations. Information is never sold.

Data retention and security

  • Server logs are kept only as long as operationally needed. Market data shown is public and contains no personal information.

User accounts

  • The site currently offers no user accounts or login. Journal notes on the Tools page are stored only in your own browser (localStorage) and are never transmitted to our servers.
