Scalp Model — HLC 3-Bar Alignment

Signal generation

Signal fires when 3 consecutive 5-minute bars all move the same direction across high, low and close: each bar's high, low and close all rise (bullish) or all fall (bearish) versus the previous bar. Evaluated on the full 5-minute history, so alignment can span sessions.

Entry

Entry at the open of the candle immediately after the third aligned bar. No lookahead.

Exit

Stop = signal candle low − 1 (bull) / high + 1 (bear). Target = entry ± 1.5 × risk. Otherwise flat at 15:20 open. Underlying-price measures only.

No Telegram/social alerts. Live trades persisted by watcher; EOD backtest logged daily.
