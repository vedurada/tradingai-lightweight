How a signal is generated

Deterministic, read-only evaluation on completed 5-minute candles. No lookahead, one trade per instrument per day. See methodology and CPR guide.

Flowchart

1. Session gateWeekday, 09:15-15:30 IST, not holiday/weekend. Else NO TRADE.
▼
2. Data gatesCompleted 5m candle exists, feed fresh (not STALE), OHLC valid. Else NO TRADE / WAIT.
▼
3. Day levelsPrev session H/L/C - TC, BC, Pivot, R1/R2, S1/S2, PDH/PDL.
▼
4. Trigger scan (bear first)BEAR: high at/above R2, R1, PDH, or close below BC. BULL: low at/below S2, S1, PDL, or close above TC. First trigger of day wins. None gives no_cpr_trigger - WAIT.
▼
5. Weekly gate (aligned)BEAR needs close below weekly BC. BULL needs close above weekly TC. Fail - skip candle.
▼
6. Entry gateEntry = next candle OPEN in [09:20, 15:20). Missing gives entry_at_next_open - WAIT. Stop 0.5%, target 2% (RR 4.0). BEAR is Bear Call Spread, BULL is Bull Put Spread.
▼
TRADEsignal_time = trigger candle, entry_time = next open (+5m). Alert on next 5-min poll (+5m). So signal to alert is about 10m.
