Potential resistance / potential support
Expected move / expiry context
PCR (Put-Call Ratio) compares total Put open interest to Call open interest. Above 1.5 = put-heavy (bearish); below 0.7 = call-heavy (bullish). Measures capital allocation, not direction.
Max Pain The strike at which the maximum number of option holders lose money at expiry. There is no reliable evidence that price converges to it; treat as context, not a forecast.
Signal Confidence Measures how strongly indicators agree — not a probability of being correct.
OI Concentration Strikes with highest Call and Put open interest, marking potential resistance (call) and support (put) zones. OI change data from NSE live feeds.
