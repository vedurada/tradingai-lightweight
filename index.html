Options Intelligence

Signal Confidence ⓘ — measures agreement among market signals; not a probability of being correct.

PCR (Put-Call Ratio) compares total Put open interest to Call open interest. A PCR above 1.5 suggests put-heavy positioning (often bearish sentiment); below 0.7 suggests call-heavy (bullish). Values between 0.7 and 1.5 are considered balanced. PCR does not predict direction alone — it measures where capital is allocated.

Max Pain is the strike at which the maximum number of option holders would lose money at expiry. Max Pain is a theoretical expiry reference derived from option open interest. Some traders watch it as a possible magnet level, but there is no reliable evidence that price converges to it; treat it as context, not a forecast. It is a short-term reference, not a guarantee.

Expected Move estimates the one-standard-deviation price range by expiry based on implied volatility — about 68% of the time price stays within this range. It is not a forecast of direction.

OI Concentration highlights strikes with the highest Call and Put open interest, marking potential resistance (call concentration) and potential support (put concentration) zones. OI change data is available from NSE live feeds; yfinance-based sources may show zero change and are flagged as temporarily unavailable.

Confirmation measures alignment between market direction and options positioning. CONFIRMED means market and options agree; DIVERGENCE means they disagree; PARTIAL CONFIRMATION means one is stronger than the other. Options view derives from Max Pain vs spot, PCR, and OI concentration — never from LLM.

How to read this page

TradingAI is a free, fully automatic market-intelligence service for the Indian stock market. This home page is the AI Market Outlook: a live dashboard for NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, FINNIFTY and SENSEX. Use the tabs above to switch index. Every section — the market regime, probability bars, 8-factor scoring grid, options intelligence, strategies, key levels and AI decision — comes from the same automatic pipeline: publicly available NSE data in, structured analysis out.

The sections reinforce one another. The regime tells you the macro posture. The probability bars quantify directional bias. The 8-factor grid breaks down why the AI holds that view — from trend and momentum to VIX and breadth. And the options intelligence summarises where the big money sits. Reading these together is more informative than any single number.

During market hours the page refreshes on a fast cycle, and price, levels and PCR figures are recomputed from the live feed. The AI Decision box labels the trade or no-trade verdict the model would take under the current regime — and it will honestly say WAIT when signals conflict.

We publish this for free and keep the methodology transparent. Every number is computed server-side from stored candles, so the same page works offline or shows historical context whenever the market feed is delayed. Nothing here is investment advice, and every decision to trade remains yours.
