How to read this page

TradingAI is a free, fully automatic market-intelligence service for the Indian stock market. This home page is the control room: from here you can reach the intraday outlook for NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, FINNIFTY and SENSEX, the broad-market dashboard, an equity scanner, options strategies, PCR and max-pain analysis, mutual fund snapshots, and a growing education library.

Every number on the site is computed from public Indian market data — index levels, option open interest, breadth, VWAP, RSI, ADX and the India VIX — by a rule-based pipeline. Those raw figures become a 'regime' (trending up, trending down, range-bound or volatile) and that regime drives the strategies you see on the index pages. No editorial opinion is involved: what you see is a reproducible reading of the data at that moment.

The portal cards below update through the trading day. During market hours they refresh on a fast cycle so the live prices visibly blink whenever a quote ticks up or down; after the close they settle to the day's final values. Because everything is computed server-side from stored candles, the same page works offline or shows historical context whenever the market feed is delayed.

We publish this for free and keep the methodology transparent. If a level, NAV or scanner row looks wrong, tell us via the contact page — data pulls from public feeds are occasionally corrected and we audit them regularly. For context on how we interpret indicators, see the Learn hub; for the legal boundaries of this information, see the disclaimer and terms. Nothing here is investment advice, and every decision to trade remains yours.

TradingAI provides market analysis and decision-support information. Market conditions can change rapidly. No strategy or outlook guarantees profit. Verify market data and consider your own risk tolerance before trading.
