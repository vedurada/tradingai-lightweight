PCR Explained: Put-Call Ratio

What PCR is

Total put open interest (or volume) divided by total call open interest. PCR above 1 means more put positioning than call positioning, and vice versa.

Reading it

Rising PCR with a rising market = hedged longs (healthy). PCR above ~1.3 often marks fear extremes that precede bounces; PCR below ~0.7 marks complacency that precedes falls.

OI vs volume PCR

OI-based PCR reflects positioning; volume PCR reflects today's urgency. TradingAI uses OI PCR for bias and volume PCR for confirmation.

Limits

PCR alone never triggers a trade on TradingAI — it is one vote alongside regime, VWAP, breadth and VIX.
