How to read this page

The news page gathers today's market headlines, pre-market cues and corporate actions from public sources and organises them around the symbols and themes TradingAI tracks. It is the 'what moved, and why' layer that turns raw price data into narrative context.

Reading markets well means triangulating the numbers with the news. A big NIFTY gap usually has a catalyst in the morning cues; a stock pinned at its day high often connects to an announcement in the news list. Keep the market dashboard and an index page open alongside this feed so price behaviour and headlines speak to each other.

Corporate actions matter mechanically: dividends, splits, buybacks and rights issues change price series and can distort technical signals. We surface these so you can adjust your read of a chart instead of being surprised by an ex-date gap. Our data-quality and regime pages explain how these events are handled in the pipelines.

Headlines open to external sources under their own terms, and we do not vouch for their accuracy. The page aggregates public information for research; it is not advice, and reacting to a headline alone is rarely a sound strategy. Verify facts before acting.
