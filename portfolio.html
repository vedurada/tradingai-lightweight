How to read this page

The Portfolio page is a personal journal for your NSE trades: every position you log here gets a live comparison against the current market price, so you can see unrealised gain or loss without opening your brokerage app.

For a LONG position your P&L is positive when the live price is above your entry price; for a SHORT position the opposite holds. The tracker uses the same delayed market feed as the rest of the site, so treat the figures as estimates for research and planning, not as a replacement for your broker statement.

Log the strategy name for each trade — after a few weeks the tracker doubles as a mini performance diary, letting you see which approach (OI breakout, breakouts, options spreads) actually earns. Empty rows can be removed with the delete button.

This tool is for record-keeping and research. Nothing on the page is advice. Always verify prices with your broker before acting.
