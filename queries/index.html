How to read this page

The Queries hub is a set of live, askable market questions — top performers, biggest gainers and losers, sector strength, unusual volume, and other custom screens that would normally require a dedicated deal. Each query is computed from current market data on every visit, so the answers are the market's, not a static list.

Using queries well is about asking sharper questions. Instead of 'which stocks are rising', ask 'which stocks are rising *on volume* in a failing tape?' or 'which sectors are leading while the index drifts?'. The hub's filters let you combine conditions so the output matches a thesis rather than merely confirming one.

Each query card links back to the scanner and to individual stock pages, so a good answer becomes the start of deeper research rather than the end of it. Queries also complement the news page: unusual volume often precedes an announcement, and the two layers together frame the day.

Queries are computed automatically from public feeds and can be delayed; a ranking is a moment-in-time snapshot, never a forecast. They are tools for research, not reasons to trade. Verify any name you act on with current data and your own analysis.
