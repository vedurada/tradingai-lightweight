## Phase B — Entry Timestamp Audit

See `ENTRY_TIMESTAMP_AUDIT.csv`.

### Results

- 729 trades audited
- All entry prices available after signal detection (historical data)
- 729/729 entry timestamps >= signal candle end
- Max delay: 63,900s (17.75h) — overnight signal (Jul 7 15:25 → Jul 8 09:15)
- Min delay: 0s (same-candle entry, next candle starts immediately after)

### Finding: Overnight Signals

Some signals fire on the last candle of a trading day (15:25) and enter on the first candle of the next day (09:15). This is a 17-hour delay. In live trading, the entry guard would check if the entry candle (next day 09:15) is within 5 min of now. At 09:15, it passes. But the signal is 17 hours old. The `is_stale` check uses entry_time (today 09:15), not signal_time (yesterday 15:25), so it passes. This means overnight signals can create trades in live trading.
