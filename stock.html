How to read this page

This page is the full TradingAI AI Market Outlook for the stock you asked for (use ?symbol=RELIANCE or ?symbol=ADANIENT). Every section — the market regime, probability bars, 8-factor scoring grid, options intelligence, strategies, key levels and AI decision — comes from the same automatic pipeline: publicly available NSE data in, structured analysis out. Add the symbol to the URL to switch names, e.g. RELIANCE, SBIN, TATASTEEL.

The sections reinforce one another. The regime tells you the macro posture. The probability bars quantify directional bias. The 8-factor grid breaks down why the AI holds that view — from trend and momentum to VIX and options positioning. Reading these together is more informative than any single number.

During market hours the page refreshes on a fast cycle, and price, levels and options figures are recomputed from the live feed. The AI Decision box labels the trade or no-trade verdict the model would take under the current regime — and it will honestly say WAIT when signals conflict.

This is automated research, not advice. Stock prices can gap and individual names carry single-stock and earnings risk. Verify data with your broker and decide for yourself.
