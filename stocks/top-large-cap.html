How to read this page

Top Large Cap ranks the largest, most liquid stocks TradingAI follows by live performance, so you get an instant read on how the heavyweight names — the ones that steer NIFTY — are behaving today.

Large-caps drive index moves, so this listing is effectively the market's engine room. When the top of the list is broadly green on strong breadth, index upside tends to be more durable; when a few names carry the whole index while most drift, the move is fragile. Reading the performance split tells you whether strength is real or concentrated.

Each row links to the full research page for that stock and to the scanner for the momentum context, so a strong (or weak) performer becomes a starting point for investigation rather than a one-line number. The Learn hub explains how to read performance rankings with volume and relative strength.

The list is computed from public market data at the time you load it — a snapshot with typical feed delays, not a recommendation. Large-caps are 'safer' than small-caps but they are still equities carrying real risk. Verify levels and do your own diligence before trading.
