How to read this page

The Today page is a fast summary of where the market sits right now: the closing snapshot of the major indices, today's key events, and links into the deeper research layers of the site. It is built to answer one question quickly — 'what happened today, and what should I look at next?'

The page emphasises the session's result because context matters: a 0.3% NIFTY move on a low-volatility day is a different story than the same move on a VIX spike. The index strips update through the trading day and settle at the close, and each strip links to the full outlook page for that instrument.

Use Today as a daily habit: glance at the closes, note how breadth behaved, then spend your real attention on the index pages that matter to the positions you hold or the sectors you trade. Consistent daily reading builds a mental model of the market's character that static articles cannot.

The content is automatically assembled from public market data for information and research. It is not advice, and a day's headline move is never a reason by itself to trade. Verify data before acting.
