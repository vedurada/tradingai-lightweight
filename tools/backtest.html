Choose a symbol and period, then run a backtest over the strategy engine's logged trades.

How to read this page

The Backtest tool runs straight over the trades the strategy engine has logged in the history ledger — the same ledger that drives the P&L history page. Each day's session is a single trade with an entry, an exit and a WIN/LOSS/FLAT result in points.

The headline numbers are the classics of performance analysis. Win rate tells you how often the engine is right; profit factor (total winning points divided by total losing points) tells you whether the size of the winners covers the losers. A profit factor above 1.0 is profitable, but below 1.5 most traders would call it noisy. Max drawdown is the largest fall from a peak in cumulative points — your true worst stretch, and the number to size positions around.

Consecutive win and loss streaks answer the practical question nobody asks about reported strategies: how bad can a bad patch get? Knowing the worst recorded runway is useful when you size a single trade. The per-strategy breakdown shows which named plays (OI breakout, gap-fade and so on) are earning their keep and which ones to demote.

The backtest reflects logged strategy history on a live index, past performance is not a guarantee of future results, and the sample is short. Use the insight to size, not to gamble.
