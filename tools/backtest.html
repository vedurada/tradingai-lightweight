How to read this page

Win rate & profit factor tell you how often the engine is right and whether winners cover losers. PF >1.5 is healthy.

Equity curve & drawdown show the path of capital — recovery time and worst month matter more than a single peak number.

Market-condition table splits the same trades by regime so you know where the edge lives (and where it doesn't).

Points are the engine's native unit; ₹ assumes ₹75/pt on your starting capital and is explicitly labelled.
