How to read this page

Total Trades includes all opened trades. Completed excludes still-open positions.

Profit Factor = gross wins / gross losses. Above 1.5 is healthy.

Average R = average R-multiples of completed trades. Above 0.5R is viable.

All costs are explicit — brokerage, slippage, and exchange charges are included in Net P&L.

AI is excluded from all calculations. Results are fully deterministic.

Historical evidence only. Not a prediction or guarantee.
